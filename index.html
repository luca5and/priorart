1Describe

Write your idea in a few sentences. Each sentence is treated as one feature.

2Search

Your text is matched against titles, abstracts and claims, by keywords and by meaning, then re-ranked with a model trained on patent examiners' citations.

3Compare

For the top results, each of your features is matched to the closest part of the patent's first claim: strong match, partial match or not found.

About

A free, independent search tool in beta. The current index covers consumer products: household and kitchen, personal items, sports, toys, pets and fishing, hand tools and packaging (CPC A47, A45, A63B, A63H, A01K, B25B–H, B26B, B65D). More technical fields will follow.

Privacy

Your text is sent over an encrypted connection, used only to run your search, and never stored or logged. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking, and no third-party AI service sees your text.

Data

Patent data from Google Patents Public Data by IFI CLAIMS Patent Services and Google, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Links open the full documents on Google Patents.

This tool is a search aid, not legal advice. Coverage is limited to the indexed documents with English text, so a search without close results does not mean an idea is new. Treat anything you must keep confidential before filing with the same care as with any online service.
